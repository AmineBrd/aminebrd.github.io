This blog is my public notebook for cybersecurity.

The goal is simple: learn by doing, write while the lesson is fresh, and turn scattered commands into a system I can reuse.

## My Starting Roadmap

1. Linux fundamentals and command-line fluency.
2. Networking basics: TCP/IP, DNS, HTTP, SMB, LDAP, Kerberos.
3. Web security: authentication, injection, file upload, SSRF, deserialization.
4. Active Directory: enumeration, common misconfigurations, certificates, Kerberos.
5. Defensive thinking: logs, detections, hardening, and incident notes.

## How I Will Write

Every writeup should answer four questions:

- What did I see?
- What did I try?
- What worked?
- What should I remember next time?

I do not want perfect posts. I want useful posts. The best note is the one that helps me solve the next machine faster.
